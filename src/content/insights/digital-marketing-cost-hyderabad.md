## Why this is hard to find out

Search for what digital marketing costs in Hyderabad and you will find ranges, not prices.
"Depends on your goals." "Starting from a custom quote." "Let's get on a call."

Some of that is genuine — scope really does change the number. Most of it is not. An unpublished
price means two discovery calls happen before either side finds out the budget was never going to
match, and it means the proposal arrives as a single figure you have no way to interrogate.

So here are ours, with what sits inside them. Every figure below also appears on the
[pricing page](/pricing), and both are generated from the same file, so they cannot drift apart.

## The three monthly packages

Growth marketing is sold as a monthly fee. Every figure below excludes GST at 18%, and excludes the
ad budget, which is discussed further down.

| Package | Monthly fee | Built for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local Visibility | ₹20,000 | New businesses, local shops and early-stage startups that need to be found in Hyderabad |
| Search & Social Growth | ₹40,000 | Scaling brands, D2C businesses and growing SMEs buying search and social together |
| Market Leader | ₹60,000 | Brands that want to own their category in search results and in AI answers |

**Local Visibility** covers Google Business Profile setup and optimisation, [local SEO](/services/search) across
five to ten keywords, social media on two platforms, eight to twelve posts a month including Reels, one
managed paid campaign, and a monthly performance report.

**Search & Social Growth** widens that to full on-page and off-page SEO across fifty keywords,
[Google Ads](/services/paid-search) and [Meta Ads](/services/paid-social) both fully managed, three to four social platforms, twenty to twenty-five posts a
month, two to three influencer collaborations, an AI chatbot for lead capture on your site, and
fortnightly strategy calls against a live dashboard.

**Market Leader** adds [answer-engine and AI-search coverage](/services/search) on top of advanced SEO, extends paid into
LinkedIn and YouTube, runs thirty to forty posts plus one or two brand videos a month, five to ten
influencer campaigns, and gives you a dedicated account manager with weekly calls.

Above that, custom scopes run up to ₹1,00,000 a month.

## What sits on top of the fee

Two things, and both of them catch people out.

**GST at 18%.** ₹20,000 is ₹23,600 paid. ₹40,000 is ₹47,200. ₹60,000 is ₹70,800. If you are a
registered business this is usually input credit rather than cost, but it is the number that leaves
your account.

**Ad spend, paid by you.** The fee buys the work. The budget that buys the impressions is paid
directly to Google, Meta or LinkedIn from accounts in your own name. It never passes through us, and
it is never marked up — which also means we have nothing to gain from telling you to spend more.

| Package | Fee inc. GST | Ad budget | Total monthly outlay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local Visibility | ₹23,600 | ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 | ₹28,600 – ₹38,600 |
| Search & Social Growth | ₹47,200 | ₹20,000 – ₹50,000 | ₹67,200 – ₹97,200 |
| Market Leader | ₹70,800 | ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000 | ₹1,20,800 – ₹1,70,800 |

Those ad budget floors are not arbitrary. Below roughly ₹5,000 a month a campaign never gathers
enough conversion data to optimise against, and you end up paying a management fee to run something
that cannot learn.

## How that compares with the rest of Hyderabad

Two agencies in this city do publish figures, and both were checked on 16 September 2026.

[BeTopSEO's Hyderabad cost guide](https://betopseo.com/blogs/digital-marketing-cost-hyderabad/)
(Henry Lewis, 19 April 2026) puts a small local business at ₹15K–₹40K a month, a mid-size
business at ₹40K–₹1L, and an enterprise or local category leader at ₹1L–₹2L+. It prices SEO
alone at ₹10K–₹25K a month for a basic scope and ₹25K–₹75K for an advanced one.

[Glamark's pricing guide](https://glamark.in/digital-marketing-cost-hyderabad/) (updated
18 April 2026) publishes three monthly bundles — ₹24,999, ₹49,999 and ₹99,999 — and, like us,
states that all prices exclude 18% GST and that ad spend is billed separately and paid directly
to the platforms.

Akvega's ₹20,000, ₹40,000 and ₹60,000 sit inside both of those ranges rather than under them.
This is not a claim to be the cheapest option in Hyderabad, and if that is what you are
optimising for you will find it. The difference worth paying attention to is what the fee
contains: whether technical fixes are implemented or handed to you as a list, and whether the
people running the campaign can change the page it points at.

Treat all three of these as what they are — published rate cards, not a market survey. Nobody
has audited what Hyderabad agencies actually invoice, ourselves included.

## What build work costs

[Websites](/services/websites), [online stores](/services/commerce), [mobile apps](/services/mobile),
product design and internal tools are quoted per project rather than per month, and we do not publish a starting figure for them yet.

That is a deliberate gap rather than an oversight. A "websites from ₹X" number is accurate for
exactly one shape of website and misleading for every other, and the honest version of the answer
needs to know how many pages, whether you need to edit them yourself, whether the content and
photography exist, and what has to integrate with what. The [paid diagnostic](/paid-diagnostic) establishes
that, and the quote comes out of it.

## What actually moves the number

If you are comparing quotes — ours or anyone's — these are the variables doing the work:

- **How many keywords, and how competitive.** Five local terms and fifty national ones are not the
  same job.
- **Whether content is written or only recommended.** A great many SEO quotes cover the audit and
  the plan, and stop at the point where somebody has to write the page.
- **Whether technical fixes are implemented or handed over.** A list of problems is cheaper than
  fixed problems. It is also worth much less.
- **How many channels.** Each platform added is another creative format, another audit, another
  reporting surface.
- **Whether there is a build attached.** A campaign pointing at a page nobody can change on your
  behalf is the most expensive kind of cheap — which is the whole argument for
  [one team holding both ends](/one-team-for-growth-and-build).
